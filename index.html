🌄 Kerala → Ooty Day Trip

Kedavur • Pykara • Ooty • Back the same night

🚗 Start Full Route in Maps 📍 Start Point
⏰ Timing tip: Pykara Waterfall opens around 8:30 AM, but the Boat House counter opens around 10:00 AM. Visit the waterfall first so nobody waits at the gate.

🎒 Quick Tips

  • Carry a jacket or sweater. Ooty is cold, especially in the evening.
  • Keep cash handy for boat tickets, parking and horse rides.
  • Fill up fuel and take a rest before the night drive back.
  • Share live location with family on the return drive.
  • Opening times can change, so check on the day.
Happy journey! 🌿 Plan shared for family use.
